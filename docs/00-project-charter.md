# Project Charter

## Purpose

Ragged Claws exists to determine whether publicly observable economic behavior can be converted into durable investment signals.

The project studies capital movement, ownership, incentives, institutional relationships, government activity, and other public evidence surrounding publicly traded companies.

The objective is not to explain the world.

The objective is to make better investment decisions.

## Core Research Question

> Given only information publicly available at time T, can observable changes in the behavior of informed, connected, or economically incentivized actors identify securities with materially different forward risk-adjusted returns?

## Working Hypothesis

Economic actors respond to incentives, constraints, information, and opportunity.

Those responses frequently create observable records.

Examples include:

- securities filings
- insider purchases and sales
- ownership disclosures
- government awards
- lobbying filings
- campaign-finance records
- board and executive changes
- institutional holdings
- political financial disclosures
- market transactions

Individually, many of these observations may contain little useful information.

Ragged Claws tests whether particular events, relationships, or combinations of signals predict forward excess returns.

## Unit of Analysis

The project should avoid assuming that the named person on a filing is necessarily the economically relevant actor.

Where appropriate, analysis may operate at the level of:

- individual
- household
- beneficial owner
- trust
- partnership
- fund
- controlled entity
- corporation
- institutional network

Attribution confidence must be preserved explicitly.

## Intended Investment Style

Ragged Claws is designed around:

- long-only positions
- shares or fractional shares
- medium-term holding periods
- low turnover
- selective deployment
- benchmark-relative evaluation
- no requirement to remain fully invested

The system may observe thousands of events while recommending very few investments.

That is desirable.

## Initial Capital Constraint

The initial live strategy is expected to receive approximately:

**$500/month in new investment capital**

This amount is a research constraint, not a target architecture.

The model should be designed to discover durable signals rather than optimize specifically for a small account.

## Success

Ragged Claws succeeds only if it produces repeatable evidence of incremental value relative to appropriate passive benchmarks.

Raw profit alone is insufficient.

Evaluation should distinguish:

- absolute return
- market-relative return
- sector-relative return
- risk-adjusted return
- transaction costs
- data costs
- infrastructure costs

## Non-Goals

Ragged Claws is not intended to:

- prove corruption
- determine criminal liability
- judge whether conduct is ethical
- expose political actors
- advocate for political positions
- predict elections
- generate constant trading activity
- become an intraday trading system
- manufacture signals where none exist
- reproduce vendor scores without independent testing

Questions of legality, morality, and motive may sometimes provide useful context.

They are not the project's primary research objective.

## Decision Standard

The default response to uncertainty is:

**collect more evidence or do nothing.**

Capital deployment requires evidence.

Curiosity does not.
