# Research Philosophy

> “Show me the incentive and I will show you the outcome.”  
> — Charlie Munger

Ragged Claws begins here.

People respond to incentives.

Companies respond to incentives.

Funds respond to incentives.

Government agencies, executives, directors, households, lobbyists, investors, and institutions respond to incentives.

Those incentives influence behavior.

Behavior leaves evidence.

Some of that evidence is public.

Ragged Claws exists to determine whether that evidence is useful for investing.

## No Grand Theory Required

The project does not begin from a theory that markets are rigged, institutions are honest, elites conspire, regulators are ineffective, or public markets are perfectly efficient.

None of those beliefs is required.

Ragged Claws assumes only that:

1. incentives influence behavior;
2. information and access are distributed unevenly;
3. actors reveal information through their behavior;
4. public records capture some portion of that behavior;
5. markets may incorporate different classes of information at different speeds.

Those propositions can be tested without assigning moral meaning to them.

## Behavior Before Narrative

An insider purchase is observable.

A spouse-owned securities transaction is observable.

A government contract is observable.

A lobbying filing is observable.

A board appointment is observable.

An institutional ownership change is observable.

Why an actor took an action may not be observable.

Ragged Claws therefore separates:

**observation**
from
**interpretation**

and separates both from:

**predictive value**

The project should never need to call an event suspicious, corrupt, unethical, or illegal in order to test whether it predicts future returns.

## Legality, Ethics, and Predictive Value Are Separate Questions

An event may be:

- legal and predictive
- legal and useless
- ethically controversial and predictive
- ethically controversial and useless
- illegal and predictive
- illegal and useless

Ragged Claws is principally concerned with the final dimension:

**Does the observable information improve investment decisions?**

The other dimensions should only enter the model where they can be supported by evidence and materially improve analysis.

## Power Is Context, Not Signal

Relationships matter because they may explain access, incentives, information flow, capital allocation, or institutional exposure.

A prestigious affiliation, political relationship, elite social connection, board seat, or government role is not itself an investment thesis.

The project should distinguish:

**network structure**
from
**economic activity**

A relationship becomes more interesting when paired with observable behavior.

For example:

```text
relationship
+ capital movement
+ economically relevant event
+ incomplete market repricing
```

may warrant investigation.

A relationship alone usually should not.

## Convergence Over Celebrity

Ragged Claws should resist celebrity-driven analysis.

The identity of a famous politician, executive, billionaire, investor, or fund manager may make an event interesting enough to investigate.

It should not make the event predictive by definition.

The preferred unit of evidence is convergence among independent observations.

The data must determine whether convergence actually matters.

## Markets Are the Judge

A compelling explanation is not enough.

A signal must survive:

- timestamp-safe historical testing
- appropriate benchmarks
- regime variation
- out-of-sample validation
- forward observation
- realistic execution assumptions

If it does not, discard it.

No amount of narrative interest rescues a signal that does not produce useful investment information.

## Practical Objective

Ragged Claws is not trying to understand everything.

It is trying to find repeatable situations in which publicly observable evidence changes the expected distribution of future returns.

If those situations exist, the system should identify them.

If they do not, the system should say so.

The objective is simply to make money.

Leave all symbolism to the symbol-minded.
